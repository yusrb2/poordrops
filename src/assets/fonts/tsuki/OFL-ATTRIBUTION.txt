Tsuki Display Font Fallback Notice
=================================
The commercial "Tsuki" typeface is proprietary and incompatible with a GNU GPL-3.0 offline bundle.
Per project specification, this directory bundles Tsukimi Rounded (SIL Open Font License 1.1)
from @fontsource/tsukimi-rounded locally in WOFF2 format under the local @font-face family 'Tsuki'
so PoorDrops works 100% offline with zero external CDN requests.
